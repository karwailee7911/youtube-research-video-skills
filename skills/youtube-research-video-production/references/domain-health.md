# Health and medicine evidence rules

Use this file only for health or medical topics.

## Source order

Prefer:

1. Current clinical guidelines and diagnostic classifications
2. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
3. Primary clinical or epidemiological studies
4. Authoritative public-health organizations

Use ancient medical texts as historical primary sources when relevant. Record the work, chapter, edition, page, full passage, and OCR uncertainty. Do not translate an ancient term directly into a modern diagnosis without evidence.

## Required distinctions

- Diagnosis versus symptom
- Association versus causation
- Acute treatment versus prevention
- Professional treatment versus self-treatment
- Statistical significance versus meaningful patient benefit
- Ancient treatment context versus modern home use

Record population, intervention or exposure, comparison, endpoint, effect size, follow-up, adverse events, and limitations.

## Safety

Include red flags and care boundaries when the topic involves symptoms or treatment. Do not prescribe, diagnose a viewer, teach invasive self-treatment, or hide contraindications. Place major limitations next to the related claim.

Use a proportionate medical-information disclaimer in the upload description.
