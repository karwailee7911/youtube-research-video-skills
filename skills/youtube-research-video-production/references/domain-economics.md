# Economics and business evidence rules

Use this file only for economics, markets, policy, or business topics.

## Source order

Prefer:

1. Official statistics and named datasets
2. Central banks, ministries, regulators, and company filings
3. Reproducible academic research
4. Credible industry data with disclosed methods

Record release date, reference period, geography, unit, frequency, revisions, seasonal adjustment, and source table. Preserve the original download or screenshot before annotation.

## Required distinctions

- Nominal versus real values
- Level versus rate of change
- Stock versus flow
- Correlation versus causation
- Forecast versus observed result
- Aggregate average versus distribution
- Company claim versus audited or regulatory disclosure

Show denominators and comparison periods. Recalculate headline percentages when the raw numbers are available. Mark estimates, revisions, and methodological breaks.

Avoid personal financial advice unless the user explicitly requests it and appropriate current verification is performed.
